## Developer Certificate of Origin (DCO)

All commits must be signed off under the
[Developer Certificate of Origin 1.1](https://developercertificate.org/). Add a
`Signed-off-by` trailer to each commit certifying that you wrote the work, or
otherwise have the right to submit it under the project's license:

    Signed-off-by: Your Name <you@example.com>

`git commit -s` adds this line automatically.
