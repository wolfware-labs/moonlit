# Moonlit Trademark Policy

"Moonlit", the Moonlit name, and the Moonlit logo are trademarks of Wolfware LLC.

The open-source licenses covering the Moonlit source code (MIT and Apache-2.0)
grant you broad rights to use, modify, and distribute the **code**. They do
**not** grant any rights to use Wolfware LLC's **trademarks**. This policy
explains what is and isn't allowed, so the community can refer to Moonlit
clearly while avoiding confusion about what is official.

## You may, without asking us

- **Refer to Moonlit truthfully.** Say that your project "works with Moonlit",
  is "a plugin for Moonlit", is "built on Moonlit", or is "compatible with
  Moonlit" — as long as the statement is accurate and doesn't imply that we
  produce, sponsor, or endorse your project.
- **Use the name in prose and documentation** to describe the software.
- **Fork the code** under its open-source license. If you distribute your fork
  as a distinct product, give it a different name; you may still state that it
  is "a fork of Moonlit".

## You may not, without our written permission

- Use "Moonlit" (or a confusingly similar name) **as the name of your product,
  service, company, plugin, or fork** in a way that suggests it is Moonlit or is
  official, sponsored, or endorsed by Wolfware LLC.
- Offer a **hosted or managed service** using the Moonlit name or logo (for
  example, "Moonlit Cloud" or "Moonlit-as-a-Service").
- Use the **Moonlit logo** as your own, or in a modified form.
- Use the name or logo in **domain names, social media handles, or app/store
  listings** in a way that implies official status.
- Use the marks in any way that is misleading, disparaging, or that dilutes or
  tarnishes them.

## Nominative fair use

Nothing in this policy is intended to prevent lawful nominative or descriptive
use of the marks — referring to Moonlit to accurately describe your project's
relationship to it. When in doubt, make the relationship explicit (for example,
"an unofficial plugin for Moonlit").
